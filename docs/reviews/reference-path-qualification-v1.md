## Façade decision after actual proof

This actual slice works without a new host façade or Spring integration. Its repeated mechanics are
the concrete construction, qualification and pin/plan/attempt association. A future thin façade could
hide those mechanics only after a second host integration establishes a concrete ergonomic need;
it would still preserve existing owner decisions, UNKNOWN and physical drain obligations. Current
evidence does not require a Spring dependency or public AlgorithmSlot/registry/CLI/SPI expansion.
That evaluation follows the proven path and authorizes no additional implementation.

The qualified scope remains one trusted local CPU assembly and a finite stateless float32 affine
workload. Durable restart freshness, multi-node execution, accelerators, untrusted-code isolation,
production security, general resource control and external Evidence/Verdict authority remain outside
this proof. Broader frozen-semantic contradictions still require STOP.
