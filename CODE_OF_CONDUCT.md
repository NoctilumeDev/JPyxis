# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

JPyxis welcomes rigorous disagreement about architecture, evidence, and scope. Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and honest about uncertainty.

Constructive participation includes:

- challenging a claim with evidence or a reproducible counterexample;
- distinguishing observed facts from proposals and inference;
- crediting prior work and correcting attribution;
- explaining trade-offs without attacking contributors;
- preserving the project's stated proof boundary;
- acknowledging when a question remains unresolved.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation of evidence, publishing private information, or presenting planned work as implemented.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces represented as part of JPyxis.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and interactions that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions.

Reports should use a private repository contact or the maintainer contact listed on the project profile. Good-faith reports will be reviewed with attention to privacy and context.
